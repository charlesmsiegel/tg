game is the session/campaign layer of this World of Darkness app: Chronicle (campaign), Scene (a play session with live chat over both HTTP and WebSocket), Story (a narrative-arc label), Week (the XP cadence bucket), Journal, and the XP economy (WeeklyXPRequest, StoryXPRequest, XPSpendingRequest, FreebieSpendingRecord). It is fat-model Django: business logic lives on model methods, views and forms stay thin, and one async consumer (SceneChatConsumer) re-implements the synchronous post path's permission checks and roll-command parsing for real-time chat.
