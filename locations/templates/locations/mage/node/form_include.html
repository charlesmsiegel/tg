Spend them on Merits and Flaws (at cost), Size and Ratio (−2 to 2), Resonance beyond one dot per rank, and the Tass and Quintessence it generates, then choose the forms they take.

A node holds a Reality Zone equal to its rank: as many positive levels of Practices as its rank, and the same number of negative levels.
